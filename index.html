RUN SMART

A plan is guidance, not a contract.

Adjust for illness, pain, unusual fatigue, heat, hills and life. Easy running should feel conversational; threshold is controlled and comfortably hard; intervals are harder but repeatable. If you are new to running, returning from injury, pregnant, or concerned about a health condition, get appropriate professional advice before starting.
